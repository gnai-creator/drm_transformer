\section{Foundations of Directional Relational Manifolds}
\label{sec:drm_foundations}

A DRM is a geometric structure in which each point $p$ has a set of active
directions $D(p)$. Local dimensionality is defined by
\begin{equation}
  \dim_D(p) = |D(p)|,
\end{equation}
or, in linear formulations, by the effective rank of the directional set.
Unlike a classical Riemannian manifold, where local dimension is constant, a DRM
allows $\dim_D(p)$ to vary across the space. The basic structure is a pair
$(M,D)$, where $M$ is topological and $D$ is a measurable assignment of active
directions that varies continuously in an appropriate sense.

The relational metric is defined only over active directions:
\begin{equation}
  g_p: D(p) \times D(p) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}.
\end{equation}
When $D(p)$ is constant, finite, and orthonormal, the structure reduces to the
usual Riemannian case. When $D(p)$ has constant dimension and $g_p$ coincides
with the Fisher metric over a statistical family, the DRM reduces to
information geometry. In this sense, DRM may be seen as a generalization that
allows variable rank and non-orthogonal modes \cite{amari_information_geometry,
muniz_drm_v11}.

In the computational setting of this work, the directional set is not
represented explicitly as a symbolic basis, but through learned coordinates in
a manifold of dimension $d_m$ and a low-rank metric tensor. The parameterization
used in the model is
\begin{equation}
  G(x) = I + U(x) U(x)^\top,
\end{equation}
where $U(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_m \times r}$ is produced by a \emph{MetricNet}.
This form guarantees positive semidefiniteness and keeps attention cost
tractable, since the metric distance can be computed as
\begin{equation}
  d_G^2(q,k) = \|\Delta\|^2 + \|U(q)^\top \Delta\|^2,
  \quad \Delta = q-k.
\end{equation}

The toroidal convergence theorem in the original DRM theory states that, under
boundedness, recurrence, and structural stability assumptions, the dynamics may
admit compact limit sets with toroidal topology \cite{muniz_drm_v11}. In the
proposed model, this statement is not assumed as a universal guarantee; it is
treated as an empirical target testable by persistent homology.
