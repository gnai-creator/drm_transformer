\section{Topological Evaluation}
\label{sec:topological_evaluation}

The evaluation pipeline extracts DRM coordinates from a trained checkpoint and
analyzes their geometry through nine stages:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Voronoi tessellation by KMeans over DRM coordinates.
  \item Local Tangent Space Analysis (LTSA) per cell.
  \item Tangent coherence between neighboring cells.
  \item Reeb graph construction using $\gamma$, mass, or coordinate functions.
  \item Persistent homology with Vietoris--Rips complexes.
  \item Null models with shuffled or uniform coordinates.
  \item Stability analysis across KMeans restarts.
  \item Composite foliation score.
  \item Coordinate--cluster correlation by ANOVA.
\end{enumerate}

The main topological target is the Betti signature of a two-dimensional torus:
\begin{equation}
  \beta_0=1,\qquad \beta_1=2,\qquad \beta_2=1.
\end{equation}
In the implementation, we report the number of long bars in $H_1$ and $H_2$.
The strict toroidal criterion is
\begin{equation}
  H_1=2,\quad H_2=1,\quad f_{T^2}\geq 0.60,
\end{equation}
where $f_{T^2}$ is the fraction of homology restarts satisfying $H_1=2$ and
$H_2=1$.

We also report \emph{Near T2}, a diagnostic quantity that counts restarts with
$H_2=1$ and $H_1\in\{1,2,3\}$. This is not used as final validation, but it is
useful for measuring whether failures are near the toroidal signature or
topologically unrelated.
